About

Yingying is a PhD candidate in Technology and Operations Management at INSEAD. She studies how organizations can better understand and serve their customers, using empirical methods and data drawn from industry.

Before her PhD, Yingying worked across manufacturing (Volvo Group Trucks Operations), e-commerce (Casper), banking (UBS), and ridesharing (Uber), most recently as a decision scientist designing marketplace experiments at Uber. Solving real business problems across these sectors showed her that her lasting passion lies in bringing scientific rigor to problems that matter in practice, which led her to academic research.

Her research is grounded in industry data, including customer reviews in healthcare and restaurants and customer interactions with AI tools. Her work examines how congestion and urgency labels shape the way staff prioritize their workload, how firms that share a pool of customers compete and influence one another, and how AI and human agents engage customers and resolve their problems. She enjoys working at the intersection of industry and academia, turning rich operational data into insights that firms can act on.
